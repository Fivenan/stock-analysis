# Global market and asset access

Accessed **2026-10-18**. This is an access-research matrix, not a tradable list. **Trade Republic availability is UNVERIFIED for every individual instrument in this document.** A data feed listing an exchange establishes neither custody nor order eligibility. Home-market/direct access also depends on broker, account permissions, sanctions, local rules and the particular security.

## Identity and permission model

Store security identity separately from listing identity: internal instrument ID, ISIN where applicable, legal name/type/domicile, share class, listing ticker, operating/segment MIC, exchange, trading currency, economic currency exposures, primary/home listing, European listing or ADR/GDR relationship, provider mapping and mapping evidence/date. An ADR/GDR has its own identity, depositary terms, conversion ratio and fees; do not join it to ordinary shares as if interchangeable.

**VERIFIED:** ISO 10383 defines MIC identification; the [official ISO register](https://www.iso20022.org/market-identifier-codes) is the authority (accessed 2026-10-18). Its October 12 publication has an October 26 implementation date; do not apply future changes immediately. Individual MIC examples below are **illustrative / UNVERIFIED against the actual register rows**. No fabricated ISIN/ticker is offered as verified. Production mapping must retain the exact checked source and validity interval.

Required checks for every instrument: issuer prospectus and KID where applicable; exact ISIN/class; trading and settlement currency; current execution venue; broker order preview/eligibility; custody/depositary arrangements and corporate actions; bid/ask/liquidity during overlapping home sessions; tax classification; sanctions/foreign ownership and purchase limits. Until complete, mark `access_status=unverified` and keep research-only.

## FX, bonds, rates, inflation and derivatives

**Design inference:** FX reference rates support accounting/context; they are not executable bid/ask quotes. Government bond yield series are not total-return bond prices: bond backtests need price, accrued interest, coupons, maturity and default/corporate-action handling. Central-bank policy rates and inflation need publication timestamps; never use an observation period as its availability date. Bond funds, individual bonds, FX cash, futures, options and issuer certificates belong to different tax/risk/product models.

**VERIFIED framework:** [PRIIPs](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2014/1286/oj/eng), accessed 2026-10-18, requires prescribed information for relevant packaged products sold to retail investors. **INFERRED application:** US ETFs lacking a compliant KID may be unavailable to German retail buyers; exchange coverage or ticker availability does not remove that gate. It is not a blanket restriction on ordinary shares. Product appropriateness, permissions and country marketing must also be checked; no derivatives/live execution is approved for this MVP.

## Sessions, currencies and custody

**Engineering policy, not a claim of verified calendars:** use IANA time zones: America/New_York; Europe/Berlin, Paris, Amsterdam, London, Zurich, Stockholm, Copenhagen, Helsinki, Oslo, Reykjavik; Asia/Jakarta, Shanghai, Hong_Kong, Taipei, Seoul, Tokyo. Load the chosen venue's actual holidays, half-days, auctions/lunch breaks and emergency notices. A weekday-only calendar is insufficient for real-market simulation. US/European daylight-saving transitions differ; never hardcode a permanent Berlin-to-US offset. Check future timezone database changes.

Official Nordic calendar evidence is missing; the cited Nasdaq US-only page does not verify Nordic sessions or holidays. The old Xetra URL redirected to the general portal, so exact current Xetra hours remain **UNVERIFIED here**. Asian and other missing calendars block claims of realistic venue execution until independently verified.

**Illustrative accounting identity:** if one EUR buys q units of local currency, local price P has EUR value P/q. Thus EUR return = `(P1/P0)*(q0/q1)-1`. A 10% local gain with q rising from 1.10 to 1.21 gives 0% EUR return before costs. An EUR quote does not remove underlying overseas revenue/assets currency risk. Custody location, depositary fees, spreads and corporate-action handling must be checked separately from quote currency.

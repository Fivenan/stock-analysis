## What the table means for a Trade Republic user

- **Trading hours:** Trade Republic quotes most shares from 07:30 to 23:00 German time ([Trading basics](../01-trading-basics.md)), but a share is cheapest to trade and has the tightest spread while its **home market** is open. For US shares that means the afternoon and evening; for China and Indonesia it is early morning, before most people check the app.
- **Daylight-saving gaps:** Europe changes its clocks on 25 October 2026 and the US on 1 November 2026, so between 26 and 30 October the US market opens at 14:30 German time. In spring 2027 the gap is 15–26 March. China and Indonesia do not use daylight saving, so their German-time hours move by one hour twice a year.
- **Currency:** apart from Germany and the euro part of Europe, every market adds exchange-rate risk on top of share-price risk, even when Trade Republic shows the price in EUR.
- **Withholding tax:** foreign dividend tax is credited against German tax only up to the treaty rate. Anything above that (Switzerland, and Indonesia without a certificate of domicile) is lost unless you reclaim it from that country.
- **Emerging markets:** China and Indonesia are mostly reachable through ETFs. Read the specific risks on those pages: regulatory crackdowns and VIE structures for China, liquidity and the IDR for Indonesia.

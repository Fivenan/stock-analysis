## Method and credibility

Generic queries included official provider documentation/pricing; Trade Republic exchanges, costs, API and taxes; German statutes and BMF loss-offset changes; HKEX Stock Connect; official Asian statistical APIs. No private records were used in queries. Primary legislation and government agencies carry high authority for their respective rules; providers are authoritative for their products but their marketing is not independent quality evidence. Exchange listings establish products, not a German resident's broker permission. Two-source corroboration was obtained for the important derivatives loss-rule change (BMF and BFH), and ECB data syntax (help and actual response). Provider quotas generally have one authoritative source and no independent entitlement test. Community posts and third-party summaries found by search were rejected as evidence for legal/broker/API claims.

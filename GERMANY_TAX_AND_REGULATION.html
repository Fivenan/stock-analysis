## Current law and evidence

| Topic | Verified finding and practical boundary | Status / official evidence, accessed 2026-10-18 |
|---|---|---|
| Standard capital-income rate | Section 32d generally specifies 25%; statutory exceptions, assessment elections and foreign tax credits can change the outcome. Church tax interacts with the calculation; simply adding a church percentage to 25% is wrong. | **VERIFIED**, EStG 32d. |
| Solidarity surcharge | Relevant surcharge rate is 5.5% of its applicable tax base, not 5.5% of investment proceeds. | **VERIFIED**, SolzG 4. |
| Allowance | Sparer-Pauschbetrag EUR1,000, or EUR2,000 for jointly assessed spouses under the statutory conditions. This is a total annual allowance, not one allowance per broker. Section 20 covers dividends and relevant securities disposals. | **VERIFIED**, EStG 20. |
| Loss offsets that remain | Capital losses do not generally offset salary/other income categories. Share-disposal losses retain their restriction to share-disposal gains in the retrieved current statute. Carryforwards and certificates matter. | **VERIFIED**, EStG 20(6). Do not net every asset's P&L into one unrestricted pot. |
| Repealed special restrictions | JStG dated December 2, 2024 removed the special derivatives loss circle and monetary limit for claim-default losses. Old claims of a universal EUR20,000 derivatives-loss cap are obsolete for this current-law research. | **VERIFIED**, BMF 2025 changes and BFH decision. Historical/open-year treatment and bank transition corrections require case review; repeal does not remove all loss rules. |
| Funds/ETFs | Tax classification depends on the fund and investor, not its ticker or distribution policy. Private qualifying equity-fund income has 30% partial exemption; mixed-fund rate is half that. Real-estate categories differ. | **VERIFIED**, InvStG 20. A mining equity fund and commodity debt product need not share classification. |
| Vorabpauschale | Section 18 provides an annual deemed-income mechanism tied to 70% of the annual basis interest and start-of-year value, constrained by fund value growth/distributions and statutory timing rules. | **VERIFIED mechanism**, InvStG consolidated section 18. For 2026 the BMF letter of January 13, 2026 verifies **3.20% basis interest** and deemed receipt **January 4, 2027**. Other years need their own dated source. |
| Avoid double taxation at disposal | Prior Vorabpauschalen are taken into account under the disposal-gain rules. | **VERIFIED**, InvStG 19. Requires a persistent tax ledger rather than subtracting an arbitrary annual estimate twice. |
| Foreign withholding | Section 32d(5) provides conditions/limits on creditable foreign tax, including treaty reduction/reclaim implications. Gross foreign withholding is not automatically all creditable. | **VERIFIED framework**, EStG 32d. **UNVERIFIED:** specific treaty rate, beneficial-owner eligibility and reclaim procedure for any country/instrument. |
| German versus foreign broker | Section 43 establishes withholding circumstances. A foreign account does not by itself prove German tax was withheld or that annual reporting is complete. | **VERIFIED statutory framework**, EStG 43; broker-specific application **PARTIALLY VERIFIED**. |
| FX and physical assets | Securities proceeds measured in EUR, standalone foreign-currency holdings and physical assets must not be assigned one tax category automatically. Private-disposal rules in section 23 differ from capital-income rules. | **VERIFIED distinction of statutory regimes**, EStG 23; exact account/product classification **UNVERIFIED** pending facts. |
| Retail packaged products | Relevant PRIIPs sold to retail investors require the prescribed KID framework. Ordinary share ownership is not equivalent to purchasing an ETF or structured commodity debt instrument. | **VERIFIED framework**, EU Regulation 1286/2014; product applicability/current consolidated rules require checking. |

The retrieved legislation is the current online text as accessed; it is not evidence that every statement applied to every historical tax year. Do not backfill today's rules into historical tax simulations without versioning them. BMF's 2025 explanatory page describes the change; the BFH source independently identifies the December 2, 2024 statute. Precise commencement and treatment of a particular closed/open assessment, bank loss pot or correction remain case-specific checks.

**VERIFIED supplementary details**, accessed 2026-10-18: EStG 44a provides the Freistellungsauftrag mechanism for applicable withholding exemptions; the statutory allowance is not automatically multiplied across institutions. BZSt church-tax guidance identifies church-tax rate values of 8 or 9; the user's liability/rate remains unknown. BMF official 2025 income-tax handbook distinguishes gains/losses on interest-bearing foreign-currency claims/accounts under section 20 from foreign-currency private disposals potentially under section 23, and avoids double counting. Do not assert every standalone FX gain is exempt after one year or every FX balance is capital income. These are classification questions requiring exact facts.

Accumulating and distributing funds can both have taxable investment income; a distributing fund's distributions affect the annual deemed-income calculation, and accumulation is not a blanket tax exemption. Keep distribution tax, deemed annual income and disposal adjustments in separate ledger events. This follows the verified InvStG sections above; no full fund-tax ledger is claimed implemented.

## Illustrative EUR estimates

**Arithmetic assumptions, not personal advice:** taxable capital-income base EUR2,000 after permitted offsets and allowance; no church tax, no foreign credit, no special exception. Tax = 2,000 × 25% = EUR500; solidarity surcharge = 500 × 5.5% = EUR27.50; total EUR527.50. This is 26.375% of this taxable base, not of all sales proceeds. Legal rate evidence is linked above; inputs are invented teaching values.

**Fund example:** a verified qualifying private equity fund has EUR1,000 of relevant income. At a 30% partial exemption, EUR700 remains before applicable allowance/offsets. If a remaining EUR1,000 allowance covers that EUR700 and no other income consumes it, this example produces no estimated tax. Do not infer eligibility from an ETF label; source evidence is InvStG 20 above.

**Vorabpauschale teaching example:** choose an explicitly hypothetical basis interest of 2%, start value EUR10,000, end value EUR10,500 and distributions EUR50, held all year. Illustrative base amount = 10,000 × 0.70 × 0.02 = EUR140; positive growth plus distributions EUR550 exceeds it; subtracting EUR50 distributions leaves EUR90 before any qualifying partial exemption and allowance. This is not a calculation using a verified current annual basis rate. Purchase-month proration, negative years, holdings changes and statutory booking timing require complete rules/data; the MVP should not claim those are implemented.

**FX teaching example:** USD share bought for USD100 when EUR1 = USD1.10 and sold for USD110 when EUR1 = USD1.21 has unchanged EUR value before costs. This illustrates why an EUR securities gain cannot be inferred from a USD gain. It does not decide tax treatment of a separate USD cash balance or interest-bearing foreign-currency claim.

## Estimator contract and exclusions

Recommended minimum estimator inputs: tax year/rule version; realized gains supplied in EUR; relevant distributions; explicitly selected verified fund partial-exemption fraction; remaining annual allowance; permitted same-category offsets supplied by user; capital-tax rate; surcharge rate; optional church-tax setting only if correctly modeled. Tax output is `estimated`, carries assumptions and excluded features, and is never written as actual broker withholding.

Conservative narrow implementation: estimate positive supplied taxable capital income after a configurable exemption/remaining allowance with default 25% and 5.5% surcharge and no church tax. Do not auto-classify securities, currency cash, physical metals, derivatives or funds from their names. If church-tax interaction is not implemented, show “excluded,” not a mathematically incorrect surcharge. An arbitrary church rate is a scenario parameter, not verification of the user's state or liability. Annual Vorabpauschale basis rate must be an externally verified dated input or the calculation stays unavailable.

Explicit exclusions unless separately built and tested: FIFO/tax-lot accounting, multiple bank loss pots/certificates, joint assessment, Günstigerprüfung, church tax, foreign withholding credit caps/reclaims, individual treaties, fund qualification changes, actual Vorabpauschale accrual/booking, return-of-capital/corporate actions, old holdings/transition rules, business assets and standalone FX/private-disposal classification. Broad annual net P&L cannot stand in for these.

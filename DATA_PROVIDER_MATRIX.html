# Financial and macro data provider matrix

Verified on **2026-10-18**. Evidence codes and retrieval limits are in SOURCES.md. **Product documentation is not a tested free-tier entitlement.** Only ECB and World Bank underwent the small anonymous response checks recorded there. No paid service, registration or key was used. Recommendations below are engineering judgments, not guarantees.

## Reading the catalog

V = VERIFIED narrow documented fact; P = PARTIALLY VERIFIED; U = UNVERIFIED. Every row's status applies on the access date above. `Unknown` means no current validated value; it does not mean unlimited/free. Historical depth, delay, intervals and rights must be validated for each endpoint, dataset and plan, rather than inherited from a provider marketing page.

Common field defaults for **every row**, unless explicitly overridden: actual free-tier authentication test = not performed; adjusted prices/corporate actions = unknown (not applicable to macro/report-only rows); reset time and per-endpoint quotas = unknown; German-resident signup/access restriction = unverified; redistribution = not approved. Research suitability is conditional on rights, point-in-time availability and schema validation. Backtesting requires actual historical observations, corporate actions and survivorship controls. Paper fills require suitably fresh prices, not a macro series. A fallback may supply a different economic measure and must never silently replace an instrument price.

## Enablement checklist

Before adding a real connector, record exact plan and account jurisdiction; successful permitted sample response; supported instrument IDs/MICs/currency; earliest/latest observations and intervals; raw/adjusted methodology; dividends/splits/delistings; timestamps/revisions; credit weight and reset; failure payloads; caching/derived-data/redistribution permissions; Germany access limitations; documentation/access date and allowed research/backtest/paper uses. Unknown required fields block that use, rather than becoming fabricated defaults.

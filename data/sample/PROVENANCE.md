# SYNTHETIC SAMPLE
No market observations or real ticker mappings. No invented ISINs.
`gmrl.providers.sample.load_demo()` deterministically generates nine listings (US, DE, GB, JP, TW, KR, ID, CN, HK) with weekday sessions for 2024-2025 using the exact Decimal formula in that source. Jan 1 and Dec 25 are closed. Local times are deliberately simplified 09:00-16:00 using IANA venue zones and DST. These are synthetic calendars, not verified exchange calendars. DEMO-US has a 2-for-1 split on 2025-06-02; DEMO-DE pays a EUR1 dividend entitled 2025-07-01 and paid 14 days later. FX is EUR per local unit, positive, published at 00:00 UTC. Provenance includes SHA256 of canonical generated records; fixture identity is content-derived. Requires the locked timezone database on Windows.
